# LYMPHODOOM — notes for agents

Doom 1 in the browser, inside the human body: the player is a killer T cell, three levels
(lymph node, bloodstream, tumour). Published at `kostachernyshov.com/lymphodoom/` and
`kostachernyshov.ru/lymphodoom/`. How to build: `README.md`. Engine patch: `docs/engine-patch.md`.

## Rules

- Everything is generated by code: art in `art/`, maps, sound, music and data in `tools/`.
  Change the generators, never the built WAD.
- `web/game/` and `build/` are build output and are not committed. `web/` pages and images are
  committed: after changing `art/webart.py`, `art/poster.py`, `tools/page.py` or
  `web/page.template.html`, run `python3 art/webart.py web && python3 tools/page.py` and commit
  the results.
- Sprites follow the Cancer Wars character bible (palette and cast in `vendor/pixel_kit/`).
  Every departure from the bible goes into `docs/bible-addendum.md`.
- Engine changes go into the Dwasm checkout and then into `engine/lymphodoom.patch`
  (see `docs/engine-patch.md`).
- In-game text is English. The page exists in English and Russian (`tools/page.py`).
- Size budget: the whole game under 5 MB, no third-party CDNs.
- The site gets `web/` without `page.template.html` as `public_html/lymphodoom/` in the site
  repository, on a separate branch. The owner merges and publishes; agents do not push the site.

## Checking changes

- Native engine under Xvfb: `python3 tools/playtest.py <prboomX> <dir with WADs and config> <out dir> <script> -skill 2 -warp 1 1`
  (script commands are listed at the top of `tools/playtest.py`).
- Browser: serve `web/` (`python3 -m http.server -d web`) and open it in Chromium; there should
  be no errors in the console.
- Headless Chromium without a GPU runs at about 8 fps; it is not a speed test.
