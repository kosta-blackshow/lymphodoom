LYMPHODOOM needs a keyboard and a mouse. Open this page on a computer.

Start anyway

You are a killer T cell. The body is infected.

LYMPHODOOM is Doom (1993) rebuilt inside a human body. Three levels take you from a lymph node, through the bloodstream, into a tumor, where the tumor mass is waiting.

The enemies are viruses, bacteria, parasites, phages, fungal spores and tumor cells, including the ones that lost their MHC-I and are hard to see. The weapons are what an immune cell really uses: granules, cytokines, antibodies, interferon.

Game progress is saved in your browser.
